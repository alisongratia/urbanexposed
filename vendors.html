Find Your Fit

Short-Term

Market & Event Vendor

Grab a table at Record Mixer, Manchester Makers Market, or a one-off pop-up. Great for testing out a new product or just meeting the neighborhood.

  • Single-day and recurring table rates
  • Electric & load-in access included
  • Listed on the event page & social promo
Long-Term

Resident Business

Move your studio, shop, or practice into the warehouse full-time alongside our current resident artists and businesses.

  • Studio & storefront spaces available
  • 24/7 key access, shared common areas
