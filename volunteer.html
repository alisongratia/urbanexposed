Volunteer At
Warehouse 317

Mutual aid only works if people show up. Whether you've got two hours a month or two nights a week, there's a crew here for you — no experience required, just a willingness to help a neighbor.

Where We Need Hands

Volunteer Roles

Pick what fits your skills and schedule. Most crews meet monthly around our recurring events.

Hands-On

Event Setup & Breakdown

Tables, sound gear, signage, and cleanup for Record Mixer, markets, and community dinners. Heavy lifting optional, showing up is not.

Kitchen Crew

Ready? Spaghetti! Cook Team

Prep, cook, serve, and wash up for our monthly community dinner. Vegetarian and meat sauce both need a chef.

Front Of House

Greeter & Welcome Table

Be the first face people see — check people in, answer questions, and point folks toward whatever they're looking for.

Behind The Scenes

Grants & Admin Support

Help with grant research, scheduling, bookkeeping, or social media. Remote-friendly, flexible hours.

Skilled Trades

Facilities & Maintenance

Got carpentry, electrical, or general handy-person skills? A hundred-year-old warehouse always needs a hand.

Flexible

Sign up for one event or a standing monthly shift — you set the pace.

Community

Meet your neighbors, resident artists, and the rest of the Warehouse 317 crew.

Perks
