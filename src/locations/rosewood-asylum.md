  <p>Rosewood opened in 1888 as the Asylum and Training School for the
  Feeble-Minded, later renamed Rosewood State Training School, Rosewood
  State Hospital, and finally the Rosewood Center. What began as a
  progressive institution for people with intellectual disabilities became
  the site of decades of documented abuse and neglect — including a
  scandal in which patients were sent out under habeas corpus writs to
  work as unpaid servants for wealthy families. The state closed it for
  good in 2009 after years of complaints over understaffing, abuse, and
  neglect. It's since been purchased for redevelopment and awaits
  demolition.</p>
