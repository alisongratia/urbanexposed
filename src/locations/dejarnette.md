blurb: |
  <p>Founded in 1932 by psychiatrist Joseph S. DeJarnette, the sanitarium
  opened as a private institution for middle-income patients — and became
  notorious for DeJarnette's advocacy of eugenics and forced sterilization,
  a program Virginia formally apologized for decades later. The
  Commonwealth took over the complex in 1975, shifting its focus to
  treating children and adolescents with severe emotional disorders, until
  services relocated to a new site in 1996 and the original buildings were
  left empty. Demolition is currently underway.</p>
