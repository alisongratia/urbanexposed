blurb: |
  <p>The land was granted to Richard Woodson in 1748, who built a modest
  four-room wooden house that passed down through the family for
  generations. The current 14-room mansion dates to 1897, when tobacco
  businessman Walter Grey Dunnington rebuilt and expanded the home,
  engulfing the original structure entirely. He lived there until his
  death in 1922; his wife stayed until 1960. It remained a maintained
  private residence until 2000, when it stopped being lived in and was
  left to decay.</p>
