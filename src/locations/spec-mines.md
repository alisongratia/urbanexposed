blurb: |
  <p>Spec — named for the specular hematite ore buried in the mountainside
  — was opened around 1920 by the Pulaski Iron Company, which built a
  tipper, washer, and powerhouse alongside the rail line, plus about
  twenty houses for the miners and their families. Crews worked the
  mountain by hand, with little to no safety equipment, chipping out
  hematite ore for the better part of a decade. Operations shut down in
  the late 1920s; the company struggled through the Depression that
  followed, and the mining families scattered to other mines in the
  region.</p>
