blurb: |
  <p>Central State opened in 1885 on the Mayfield Farm, which the city of
  Petersburg purchased and donated to the Commonwealth — the first
  institution in the country built specifically for Black patients, and
  segregated that way until the Civil Rights Act of 1964 opened it to all
  races. Patient numbers swelled past 4,800 by the 1950s, badly
  overcrowding the ward buildings of the time.</p>
  <p>Central State still operates today, on the same campus, in newer
  facilities. But its oldest buildings — dating to the early 1900s — were
  left behind as the hospital modernized, and have sat empty for decades,
  overtaken by ivy and trees.</p>
