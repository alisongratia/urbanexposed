Day-To-Day

Staff

Founder & Executive Director

Maya Ellison

Maya started Warehouse 317 out of a single rented bay in the building, convinced Richmond needed a place where mutual aid and creative work could share a roof. Ten years later, that bay is the whole warehouse.

maya@warehouse317.org
Volunteer Coordinator

Jordan Pierce

If you've signed up to help at a Record Mixer or ladled sauce at Ready? Spaghetti!, you've probably heard from Jordan first. He keeps every crew staffed and every new volunteer welcomed.

jordan@warehouse317.org
Events & Programming Manager

Renee Castillo

Renee builds the calendar — from monthly traditions like Grindhouse Movie Night to one-off markets and workshops. If you want to bring an event idea to the warehouse, start here.

renee@warehouse317.org
Vendor & Resident Relations

Theo Banks

Theo manages the resident vendor directory and market applications — matching makers and small businesses with the right space, table, and timeline.

theo@warehouse317.org
Facilities & Operations Lead

Casey Lin

A hundred-year-old warehouse needs constant care. Casey keeps the lights on, the HVAC running, and the freight elevator honest — and leads the maintenance volunteer crew.

casey@warehouse317.org
