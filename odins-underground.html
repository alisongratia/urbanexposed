Built For Sound

Full PA and lighting rig, a stage that fits a five-piece, a proper green room, and a bar. Odin's Underground hosts touring acts and Richmond locals side by side — some nights a metal show, some nights a DJ set, some nights both.

  • Full PA, monitors & lighting rig
  • Green room & load-in from the main floor
  • Bar service for 18+ / 21+ events
